Campus Era Child Safety Standards

Campus Era is committed to maintaining a safe platform and does not tolerate child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

Our Standards

  • Users are prohibited from sharing exploitative, abusive, or sexual content involving minors.
  • Any attempt to exploit or harm minors is strictly forbidden.
  • Users violating these rules may be permanently banned.
  • Campus Era reserves the right to remove harmful content and cooperate with law enforcement authorities where legally required.

Reporting

Users can report inappropriate content or abusive behaviour through the app or by contacting us directly.
